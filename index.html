An Apology, Staged Properly, For My Baby

Act One

I know "sorry" has started to sound like a line I keep reading off the same page.

Baby, these past few days, I said things and didn't follow through. I let moments slide that mattered to you, and let you down more than once, and I understand why my word doesn't feel like something you can lean on right now.

That's not who I want to be for you. Not almost on time. Not "I meant to." Not this time will be different said one time too many. You deserve someone whose word is solid ground, not something you have to double check.

So instead of one more apology you have to take on faith, here's what I'm actually promising, out loud, in writing.
